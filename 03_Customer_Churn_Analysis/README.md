# Customer Churn Analysis

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer churn using Python and a telecommunications customer dataset. The analysis involved data cleaning, exploratory analysis, identifying churn patterns, and comparing customer characteristics between customers who stayed and those who churned.

## Tools Used

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## Work Completed

- Loaded and inspected the customer dataset.
- Checked for missing values and duplicate records.
- Identified 11 missing values in the TotalCharges field.
- Analyzed the distribution of customers who stayed and customers who churned.
- Calculated the overall customer churn rate.
- Compared average tenure between retained and churned customers.
- Compared average monthly charges between retained and churned customers.
- Analyzed churn patterns across customer attributes and services.
- Created visualizations to understand factors associated with customer churn.

## Key Results

- Total Customers: 7,032
- Customers Who Stayed: 5,163
- Customers Who Churned: 1,869
- Overall Churn Rate: 26.58%
- Average Tenure — Stayed: 37.65 months
- Average Tenure — Churned: 17.98 months
- Average Monthly Charges — Stayed: 61.31
- Average Monthly Charges — Churned: 74.44

## Key Insights

- Customers with shorter tenure showed a higher tendency to churn.
- Churned customers had higher average monthly charges than customers who stayed.
- Higher churn rates were observed among customers using certain services and payment methods.
- The analysis helped identify customer groups that showed higher churn levels.

This project helped me practice data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, data visualization, and interpreting customer behavior using Python.
